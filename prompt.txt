## How to decide which seats?

Pick the band 1 5 seats first.

Select 1 seat at random anywhere in the world.
Ths seat and its 4 peripheral (neighbouring) would become the 5 band 1 seats.

for ex. the seat chosen at random is America Isthmus (467)

now we need to do a breadth first search from there and assign remaining seats in band 5
and other bands


for ex. the 5 band 1 seats could be 467 (itself), 466, 465, 487, 486
7 seats of band 2: 458, 457, 485, 494, 493, 496, 468
and so on...

make sure that seats which are entirely made of islands who don't have a land border
with any other seat are also considered in this by modifying the adjacency graph
accordingly.

or use another distance based algorithm if u have one

from seat 467, in this way you would cover entire americas, some parts of europe, asia, etc.

Let the core seats (the very first seat that you select be distinct)
for every player. From then on, let the algorithm take its course in assigning bands.

Therefore, even in case of 10 players; these 10 core band 1 seats could be for ex.

1. America Isthmus
2. Cape of good hope
3. Chambal
4. Australia East
5. Luoyang
6. Tokyo
7. Manchester
8. Balkans East
9. Kongu Nadu
10. Khorasan

just make sure:

multiple breadth first search trees for multiple players (each player has 1 tree)
may hit the same seat; which is fine.

That is because 1 seat may have 30% committed voters to party A, 5% to B, 5% to C, etc.

this overlap is unavoidable especially when there are many players.

Therefore, you could use parallel threads for doing this BFS - 1 thread per player. i.e. 1 thread per BFS tree.
Once you have the core seats.

## How to represent these bands?

As mentioned, for colour coding:

HIGH: bands 1, 2, 3
MEDIUM: 4, 5
LOW: 6, 7

Whatever colour your party has:

The darkest shade will be assigned to HIGH band seats
then 1 shade lower to MEDIUM
and the faintest shade to LOW

when the user clicks on a seat, if the seat has committed voters of their party,
that percentage will be shown along with the band category (HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW)
