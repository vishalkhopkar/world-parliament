# Playtest notes — 2026-10-20 (AI players)

Two games on the local server, world-500 map. To review; nothing below has been changed in code or in the docx yet.

## Games
- **3NNPQY** (2 phases, 4 players: Vishal + 3 Claude agents via MCP, all nicknamed "Claude"). Compass Party (Claude via MCP) won outright with ~272 seats and did not negotiate (8 rallies + 22 roadshows; the budget was still W₹25 crore then).
- **DPXZMY** (3 phases, 10 players: Vishal + 9 Sonnet subagents Bot1–Bot9 via the CLI, budget W₹50,000). Every bot played roadshows only (125 each); Vishal was the only player who rallied (7 rallies). Bot7 formed an 8-party government as PM (left out: Bot2 and Bot4).
  - Final counts at the end of counting: Bot7 114, Bot9 82, Bot3 75, Vishal 46, Bot5 46, Bot6 44, Bot1 33, Bot2 23, Bot8 23, Bot4 14.
  - Official result (only Bot8 saw it): Bot3 and Bot9 tied at 96 points (89 seats each); Bot7 scored 0; Vishal ended with 121 seats. The cause is item 1 below.

## Decisions needed (rules → FEATURE REQUIREMENTS.docx)
1. **Elimination after the government is confirmed / after counting.** Bots stopped their CLI players during post-counting, so they were eliminated after the 90 s grace period. Under `engine/votes.ts:50` and `engine/points.ts:84`, an eliminated player's leading seats go to the runner-up and it scores 0, even as PM of a confirmed government. This overturned the government's result. Should elimination still apply once a government is confirmed or counting has ended?
   (Partly my fault: I told the bots to stop "at the very end" instead of "after status = finished".)
2. **Balance: pure roadshows dominate.** With the first-mover bonus and W₹50,000, all 9 bots went all-roadshow; the rulebook's "mix rallies and roadshows" advice no longer seems to hold. Re-run `game/sim` with the current `config/game.properties`.

## Bugs / fixes
3. **Tally and result are keyed by nickname.** In 3NNPQY three players were nicknamed "Claude": `live_tally`, `list_offers.tally` and `result.seats` merged them ("Claude: 59/63" while Compass alone had ~272). Key by player id, or enforce unique nicknames at join.
4. **`result` is null right after a government is confirmed** (in `get_state`, seen by 5 bots). It probably only arrives when post-counting ends; confirm, and maybe add an advice line ("result arrives when post-counting ends; stay connected").
5. **CLI speed.** Every `node game/agent/bin/cli.mjs` call takes several seconds (fresh node + tsx). This hurts in the lobby scramble for colours/avatars and in the short campaign. Bot6 got stuck in the lobby because its `set_party` retry loop went past the 120 s tool timeout.
6. **Undocumented caps.** `list_seats` limit ≤ 100 (default 30), `wait_for_events` timeout_s ≤ 60, `campaign_batch` ≤ 50 actions. They only appear as INVALID_INPUT errors; add them to RULES.md / the tool descriptions.
7. **Stale CLI hint.** After `start`, the CLI's "next" hint says `npx tsx agent/src/cli.ts` instead of `node game/agent/bin/cli.mjs`.
8. **Inconsistent field name.** `get_state` uses `live_tally`; `list_offers` uses `tally`.
9. **Check `phaseClosed` events with 3 phases.** Bot5 says it never received any (only `status`). A phaseClosed event did arrive in 3NNPQY (2 phases), so this is likely Bot5's filter, but worth a test.
